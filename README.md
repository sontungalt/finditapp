# Problem Statement
Students often lose their belongings at school and have difficulty finding them. The current lost and found system can be disorganised, making it difficult to identify, report and return lost items.

# Target Audience
Students, teachers and school staff who lose or find items around the school.

# Solution Overview
We will create an app where students and staff can post photos of items they find in school. Users can take a picture and add details such as the item type, colour, location and date it was found. Other students can browse the app to see items lost and found and identify their belongings. A database will store all the posts and remove items once they have been returned.

# Marketing
We will promote the app through school announcements, posters and QR codes around the school. We will encourage students to post any items they find so that their owners can easily locate them.
